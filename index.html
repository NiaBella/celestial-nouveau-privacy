Celestial Art Nouveau Watch Faces Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 August 2026

Overview

This policy covers IndigoMor's Celestial Nouveau and Celestial Four Hours Garmin watch faces. Neither face transmits, sells, shares, or stores personal data outside the wearer’s Garmin device.

Information used on the device

Depending on the watch face and the cartouches selected by the wearer, the app may access the device time, battery level, steps, heart-rate samples, calories, stress, recovery time, and Garmin's built-in local sunrise and sunset information.

This information is used only to draw the watch face, show the selected cartouche values, and choose its day, night, sunrise, or sunset artwork. It is not sent to the developer, to a server, or to any third party. The apps do not create accounts, use analytics, or retain this information.

Location

Neither watch face requests raw location permission. They use Garmin's built-in local sunrise and sunset information when it is available. If Garmin does not provide that information temporarily, they use a fixed fallback schedule solely to keep the artwork behaving predictably. Celestial Nouveau also offers manual day and night appearance choices.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to indigomor@pm.me.

Changes to this policy

If the way either Celestial watch face handles information changes, this page will be updated before the revised version is released.
