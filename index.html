Celestial Nouveau Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 August 2026

Overview

Celestial Nouveau is a Garmin watch face. It does not transmit, sell, share, or store personal data outside the wearer’s Garmin device.

Information used on the device

To display the watch face, the app may access the device time, battery level, steps, heart-rate samples, calories, and Garmin’s built-in local sunrise and sunset information.

This information is used only to show the selected information on the watch face and to choose its day or night appearance. It is not sent to the developer, to a server, or to any third party. The app does not create an account, use analytics, or retain this information.

Location

The watch face does not request raw location access. It uses Garmin’s built-in local sunrise and sunset information when that is available. If Garmin does not provide that information, the watch face uses a fallback day/night schedule. The wearer can also select an always-day or always-night appearance instead of automatic switching.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to indigomor@pm.me.

Changes to this policy

If the way Celestial Nouveau handles information changes, this page will be updated before the revised version is released.
